Welcome to the live team

Syntax State RP is live. This handbook tells every staff member how we run the server, every day.

WHY THIS DOCUMENT EXISTS

Launch day was the start, not the finish. Real players are now in the city, and every action we take affects their time and trust. A live server is only as strong as its team. When every rank works the same way, with clear powers, clear limits, and clear proof, the community sees a server that is fair, active and professional.

Duty system and daily reports

Every staff member follows the same daily cycle. It keeps work visible and the city covered.

1 CHECK IN

Post in #staff-checkin with the 2 or 3 tasks you plan to finish. Set your status to On Duty. Read #staff-chat and any handover notes.

2 ON DUTY

Handle reports and tickets, patrol, test, train and curate. Use #staff-chat for questions and #staff-help for quick help. Use admin mode only while working a case.

3 CHECK OUT

Close or hand over open cases with notes. Post your daily report in #daily-reports (management in #management-updates, curators also in #curator-reports). Set Off Duty.

ESCALATION RULE

Admin Assistant to Junior Admin, then Server Administrator, then Senior Admin and upward. Do not skip ranks unless the person above you is unreachable and the issue cannot wait. Management is contacted only through the Curator of Project, except server bugs, crashes and exploits, which go straight to the Main Developer.

EMERGENCY RULE (P1)

For a live exploit, dupe, cheating wave, leak or server crash, do not wait for the normal chain. If no Senior Admin answers within 15 minutes, contact the Curator of Project and the Main Developer directly and write down what you did in #case-log.

CHIEF ADMIN AND CURATOR OF PROJECT

The Chief Admin runs operations: daily tasks, rosters, tickets, warnings and training. The Curator of Project owns direction and final authority: goals, promotions to the top of the team, wipes and large cash actions, permission audits, staff appeals, and the right to override any admin rank with a written reason. Management is reached through the Curator of Project.

CURATORS ARE A ROLE, NOT A RANK Curator is a role given on top of your rank. Server Administrators can be assigned as State Curator or Crime Curator of an organization. Senior Admins can be assigned as Senior Curator of the State or Crime branch. Losing a curator role never changes your rank. Only the Deputy Chief Admin and above can assign or remove curators.

The 5-step case flow

1 CLAIM

Tag yourself on the ticket or report inside the response time.

2 CHECK

Spectate, read logs, hear both sides. Stay calm and neutral.

3 DECIDE

Pick the step from the sanctions guide. If unsure, ask the rank above.

4 ACT AND LOG

Save proof, write the case file, then apply the action.

5 CLOSE

Tell the player the result and a short reason. Mark the outcome.

Conduct, audit and anti-abuse

Trust is built by what we do when nobody is watching. Powers are for the server, never for us.

Admin mode rules

  • Admin mode, noclip, invisibility and admin items are only for work: a case, a test, a check. Never for fun, hiding or gaining an advantage.
  • You cannot be On Duty and play your normal character at the same time. Go Off Duty first.
  • Do not use anything you saw as staff (locations, plans, inventories, chats) in your own roleplay.
  • Never spawn items, money or vehicles for anyone, including yourself. Compensation goes through a ticket and a Senior Admin.
  • Do not interfere in a scene while spectating. Observe, record, then act if needed.

If a case involves a friend, family, your own organization or someone you are in conflict with, you must not handle it. Tell the rank above you, pass the case up, and write the reason in #case-log.

  • A "Don't know" answer counts as wrong. Read the right answer to the applicant after the interview, as training.
